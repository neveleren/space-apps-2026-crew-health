# Changelog

What changed in each version of the Crew Health Console, team Just For Fun, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Grouped from the git history; the short codes in brackets are its commits. The idea, the structure and every decision belong to the team's human member; the code was generated by Claude (Anthropic), an AI system.

## 1.1.0 (15 Nov 2026)

Fixes after a final review of 1.0.0. Separate reviewer agents found the problems; each fix was checked again by a separate checker agent, then the three branches were merged [ee4c62f, b83cdb4, f27f17c].

### Crew alarm

- A crewmate's alarm line reaches an open crew screen within seconds: every screen where someone is signed in asks the station computer every 15 s (a draft) [55a8abf].
- An urgent red limit, a camera alarm or the Help key also writes a message to the doctor by itself: to the doctor on board, or sealed in the outbox for Earth [55a8abf].
- "I am okay: false alarm" calls the alarm off on the person's own screen, and writes an all clear that takes the line away on every crew screen and tells the doctor [0c1d9c3, dbadd92, 55a8abf].
- An urgent alarm repeats its tone every 4 s and its spoken line every 16 s until someone acknowledges it; "Sound off" never mutes it and is on again at every sign-in (drafts) [55a8abf, 29ab259].
- The Help key: a single press from a screen reader or a switch asks "Call for help now?"; on the sign-in screen it raises help for this computer; the line names who pressed it [55a8abf].
- What to do keeps the true state of an alarm when the same sign is measured again later [98466bb]; a false alarm also calls off a call for help that was still waiting for the station computer [1b4fb6e].

### Saves that say what happened

- A refused message is listed as Not sent, with Send again. When the station server does not answer, what was entered waits in this browser and is sent when it answers again. Bring back a copy checks each line first and never sends old messages again [55a8abf].
- The doctor panel says before Send what will happen [6d558a4]. Signing out clears everything of the previous person [55a8abf, 98466bb]. Typed readings take a decimal comma and refuse an empty field [55a8abf].

### Medical rules (drafts)

- 35.0 °C or less is the urgent hypothermia limit (MedlinePlus, with NEWS2) [5beddbf].
- Low glucose starts with ADA's 15-15 rule [55a8abf].
- New "attention" items that ask to tell someone: an eye self-check answer other than "no change" (the flight surgeon), a symptom of 8 or more of 10 in 24 hours (the crew medical officer), a medicine past the kit list's own printed rules [dfb3400, 7484e14].

### Screens and access

- Find a measure by the name on its row [1172bf7].
- Rows, Settings and chart limit names that read on a phone; side sheets below the status line; "?" cards between the status line and the keys, never stuck in a corner [1172bf7, b5e88a6, d12905e, 9492d5a].
- The focus is kept after every redraw, focus rings are whole, notices go to one live region, and text typed in a sheet is kept as a draft on Escape [b5e88a6, 9492d5a].
- The crew's own mission dates when the example flight is off [1172bf7, b5e88a6].

### Mind support

- One word rule opens Help now in every box of the mind tools, and Help now leads to a person: a crewmate, a private call with the ground, or the plan [b086a2c, b5e88a6, 9492d5a].
- True words about who can read the journal and the plan, with Set a PIN; a journal entry is listed once; Tell the doctor on Earth goes to Earth [b086a2c, b5e88a6].

### After the merge

- Signing in failed on a leftover of the old notices; fixed [09e93ae].
- The technical view says it only shows the machine's steps; the Earth break says where its answer is kept [45b7735].

## 1.0.0 (15 Nov 2026)

Everything below came in after 0.1.0, during the night of 14 to 15 November 2026, up to commit b922945.

### Crew screen

- A calmer structure: Today shows one message and at most three things; all measures move to their own list [49ca389].
- An app shell: a status line always on top, a side menu with icons, a tab bar on phones, an icon for every measure (Lucide, ISC License) [c1f51ca].
- The measure screen shows the value and one plain sentence first; messages become one calm list [1960fff].
- A redesign: Atkinson Hyperlegible fonts, a quiet dark display with a light option, telemetry rows, "find a measure", procedures as numbered steps [eddcdae].
- "?" explanations on hover and behind a "?" key for every measure and control [28f2157]; they name the devices used on the ISS, how each value is measured and how it reaches the program [2fffb1d, f1b71bf].
- Sign-in cards that read at a glance; the phone status line fits two rows [e564060].
- The example flight follows the calendar; alert tones by level and a silence that re-arms; honest outbox wording; marks on the crew page for what the AI (SI) made [29efe5f].
- Groups with nothing to look at fold; a Systems tag with the state of the automation, AI, link, doctor, saving and sound [9924067].
- Alert tones after NASA-STD-3001 Vol 2 Table 10.3-1 and a sound test; logging a reading by hand on a measure page; saving and bringing back a copy of one's own data [dc73062].
- A mission profile in Settings: ISS, Moon or Mars sets the stated signal delay, the return options and what a message to Earth says [0e3b36e].
- A Help key, held for 2 seconds, for the manual start of an emergency response that NASA-STD-3001 Vol 2, requirement 10178, asks for (the standard wants it independent of the display; this key is on the screen); a compact status line with the flight day [f1b71bf].
- A false-alarm key on camera and Help alerts, so the person can override the automation (NASA-STD-3001 Vol 2, requirement 10165); the camera turns itself off after an hour and is not for crew quarters [2b196d4].
- "Coming up" on Today: the next private medical conference, private talk with the psychologist, thinking test or eye check (example schedule) [4fef2a1].
- "Prepare" for the weekly private medical conference: a 7-day summary drafted by the automation, edited by the person, sent only if they choose [a19d1d2].
- Word answers listed from the history; message chips wrap on a phone; the chart legend only where there is a chart [bbecd18].
- The status line fits one row on a tablet, its long words kept for screen readers [d952e5b].
- The "?" cards of exercise, the eye self-check, OCT change and images, symptoms, medicines and the check-in topics say where the value comes from, with sources, so all 38 measures now do [619ca7f]; a "?" on the private log card [a8caf86]; both file pickers (bring back a copy, import a CSV file) can be reached and opened with the keyboard [daa30b2].

### Safety and calm (drafts for the team and the flight surgeons)

- Red limits per measure from cited sources, and a direct crew alarm by automation that never needs the AI [82f0ca1].
- Calm levels: changes common in flight are information, a repeat comes before "attention", forecast alerts for slow measures, a "no fresh data" sentence and ship alarm messages [b5af997].
- The screens use them: sentence variants for fresh data and the ship, ship and missing-data chips, red items with their own steps, a crew-wide banner when a crewmate needs help, red limits and rule notes on the measure page, an exercise switch for the strap [7de327a]; rows say why an arrow carries no alert [12bafb4].
- Review fixes: a run with a red reading is never waited on; several vital signs outside at once count at once; exercise readings never change the judgement at rest [46752e0].
- An OCT change of 55 micrometres or more reaches the doctor without the AI (draft) [769cd8c].

### Measures

- More measures with checked norms; cycle ranges; English throughout [2bfb169].
- New cells after the night's NASA-style reviews, written by Claude in the roles of NASA reviewers, not by NASA: symptoms, medicines taken, exercise, eyes (SANS) [964fe00]; quick logs: a symptom in about ten seconds, a medicine in two taps [6f9a5fe]; example logs for them, with the older example stories unchanged [7dcff94].
- The machine refuses what the new lists cannot hold [ba5c259]; the example thinking tests fall on the "Coming up" days [52614cc]; honest wording on the kit list, Dose Tracker and the OCT hardware [f57a744].
- The reaction test is its own measure, apart from NASA's monthly thinking test; hearing below 0 dB HL is readable; one-tap answers are checked by the server [769cd8c].

### Mind support

- An anonymous session with AI support, built to the research guardrails: an honest name (AI support, not a psychologist), the limits of privacy before the first talk, "Help now" by simple word rules that work with the AI off, coping cards with sources, a write-it-out box that is never saved [6d7d768].
- Mind support modules: "What is weighing on you?" topics and the topic rule for the AI offer, small things that help, a private journal with a letter to the psychologist, a plan for hard days with "Talk to a person now", five-step problem solving, a mission calendar, a 3-minute reaction test after NASA's Reaction Self Test, a 3-minute Earth break with NASA photos [79143c3].
- All of it on the crew screen, with the eye self-check and its Amsler grid, the OCT draft lines and new measure groups [6d97332]; example reaction runs named as made up, never "yours" [00abbfe]; a dialog over a full-screen layer keeps its keys, and two cards say only what their source says [49b23cd]; the letter box says where the letter goes [8f127ec].

### Server, privacy and supply chain

- Static copies on the web use the browser's storage directly, with no failed server request [3c14552].
- The server protects the data: private folders in any letter case, known host names only, JSON saves from the program's own pages only, every event checked, one person per read, a sealed outbox, security headers; docs/SECURITY.md [8a2290e].
- An optional PIN per person, kept only as a salted PBKDF2 hash [1142f5e].
- SBOM.json (CycloneDX 1.6) and SHA256SUMS for vendor/ and fonts/, checked by npm test [c2efeb4].
- The server keeps the new answers in their checked shapes and passes crew alarm lines to every crewmate without the rest of the record [8835c70].
- Review fixes: the server takes what the screens send and never serves a moved data folder [96593a7]; every message to a doctor goes to the outbox, and without a key one to the doctor on board is still kept [39451ab].
- The crew screen on the hardened server: one person's records per sign-in with the optional PIN, crew alarm lines for crewmates, the page policy for the static copy, letters for the psychologist always to Earth [ee67352].
- Honest save results: a message says "sealed in the outbox" only when the server queued it, and "saved on this computer only" on a copy without the station server [769cd8c].

### Connections

- Connections with an honest status (Working, Simulated, Planned): Bluetooth thermometer, blood pressure cuff and pulse oximeter; CSV import of Hexoskin, Astroskin, Actiware and Svantek files; live ISS cabin air from NASA ISSLive and NOAA space weather as Earth demos, off by default; a corrected HL7 FHIR R4 export with Device and Provenance; an iCalendar file for "Add to plan" [d2f7107]; docs/INTEGRATIONS.md [bbfb46e].
- Review fixes: what was never tried with a real device or file says so on its row; decimal commas survive the column picker; day and month are read one way per file [bdf0cf6]; the ISSLive row says what is known about the old CO2 and O2 values [fdf7fab]; Bluetooth tests for every pulse oximeter spot-check flag [f70632e].
- On the crew screen: the Connections list in Settings, a strap without skin contact saves nothing, "I am exercising" turns itself off after 2 hours (draft) [ee67352].
- A Bluetooth measurement that will not start is skipped, and a device with none is disconnected, never left half open [b922945].

### Presentation and documents

- Seven demo slides with sources [8fd99d9], then with real screenshots: a calm day, attention, the "?" card, the AI support offer, a crewmate's alarm line and the live ISS and NOAA rows [d952e5b].

## 0.1.0 (14 Nov 2026)

The first commit [e812007], with version 0.1.0 in package.json.

- The machine (src/engine.js): every reading in its cell; number cells compared with their norm; a blood test panel checked value by value; complex data (an X-ray) to the AI for analysis, with when a doctor looks left as an open question; camera triggers; daily mind reports kept for a periodic review; the team's open questions shown as such, never guessed.

- The menstrual cycle log; the made-up example flight; history and a straight-line forecast to landing.
- A Bluetooth heart rate strap; an HL7 FHIR export with LOINC codes and the HTEST label on example data; the real Inspiration4 blood tests from NASA OSDR (OSD-575, OSD-569).
- A local server with no packages: one plain file per person in data/.
- 62 tests in 6 files.
