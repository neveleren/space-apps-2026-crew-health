## Sounds (Creative Commons 0)

Every sound was made from the page's high-quality preview (Ogg Vorbis): 40 s cut from the source, the last 4 s crossfaded with equal power into the first 4 s for a 36 s loop with no seam, then the loop's last 0.5 s put before it and its first 0.5 s after it (a 37 s file; the program loops from 0.5 s to 36.5 s, so decoding never touches the seam), resampled to 44.1 kHz stereo, levelled to about -24 LUFS (the gain below) with a peak limiter, and saved as MP3 at 128 kbit/s with no metadata beyond the encoder's name. The program plays them on a loop at 50 percent volume to start with.
