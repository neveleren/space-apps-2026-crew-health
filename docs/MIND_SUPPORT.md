# Mind support: what it is, why, and what is still the owner's call

The owner's request (15 Nov 2026): more ways to support the person's mind, built on NASA studies of recent years and on studies of support with AI. Everything below works offline and with the AI off. Since version 1.4.0 the anonymous session and "Analyse with AI" also run a real AI on this computer, with rules (see "The AI in the session" below). Code: `src/support.js`, `src/reaction-test.js`, `src/earth-break.js`, the AI in `src/ai.js`, `src/ai-rules.js` and `src/ai-view.js`, styles in `support.css`, the Earth break's videos, still frames and sounds in `assets/breathe/`. Written by Claude (Anthropic), an AI system; the owner decides.

**Kept exactly as the owner made them:** the AI offer in his words ("Hey <name>, I noticed from your check-ins that ... How about talking anonymously about what is bothering you? Or, if you wish, I can send the results of these check-ins to the doctor."; on 15 Nov 2026 he kept these first-person words), the anonymous session, the choice to tell the doctor on Earth, the private cycle tracker, the daily check-in of mood, energy and sleep. On 15 Nov 2026 he chose the Samn-Perelli seven-point fatigue scale for the energy question (1 fully alert to 7 completely exhausted), in place of the two answers "energetic" and "sluggish" (`src/fatigue.js`). Which step counts as a low day stays his open decision; the program uses 5 or more as a draft. **No questionnaire was added:** PHQ-9, PHQ-8, GAD-7 or another is his open decision.

## Guardrails built in

1. **Private by default.** Whatever the person writes goes only to the callbacks of the screen; the modules send and store nothing themselves. Journal and plan say truly who can read them: "you on your screen, and anyone who opens your screen on this computer, unless you set a PIN. Not encrypted in this prototype.", with a "Set a PIN" button that opens the PIN in Settings. Nothing is shown to crewmates or the commander.
2. **Human first.** "Help now" offers three ways to a person: talk to a crewmate now, request a private call with the ground (the line is shown before it is sent), or open my plan, and it names the Help key for immediate danger. It opens by itself when risk words are typed in the session box, the journal, the letter, the five steps or the plan: a plain word list, marked as a draft, not AI, and nothing is sent by itself. The plan is written with the crew psychologist, never by the AI; the AI never handles a hard moment alone. With the AI on, a message with risk words opens Help now first and never reaches the model, now or later in the talk; the chat shows a fixed line instead.
3. **Says what it is.** The AI notice starts with "This is an AI program, not a person." No feature is called treatment by a professional; no diagnosis anywhere. The model's own answers are checked too: a claim to be a person, a psychologist or a doctor, a diagnosis, or a medicine name or dose is held and replaced by a fixed text that names the rule.
4. **No emotion reading** from camera or voice. The camera keeps only its set physical triggers. The AI reads text only.
5. **Calm signals.** The only level any of this may raise is the blue "attention" (a clearly slower reaction test). No sounds, no red.
6. **Every card and "?" names its source** (title, year, link) and a badge: "used on the ISS", "NASA research" or "study on Earth".
7. **Offline.** Videos, still frames, sounds, icons and text are bundled; no network request. The AI runs on this computer: on board its model comes from the program's own folder; on the public demo site it downloads once from Hugging Face after the person agrees, then starts from the browser's storage.
8. **Not a fitness-for-duty test.** The reaction test says so on screen and compares only with the same person on the same computer.
9. **The automation decides.** The AI never sets or changes a level, a red limit or an alarm, and never sends or calls anyone. An answer that tells the person to ignore an alarm, or puts off action when a reading is red, is held. A red reading gets a fixed line, not AI: "A reading is past a red limit: follow the numbered steps in What to do and use Message the doctor. This line is fixed, not AI."

## The AI in the session (version 1.4.0)

- **What it is.** An open model, Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (4-bit MLC build, Apache-2.0), run by WebLLM 0.2.87 (Apache-2.0, bundled in `vendor/web-llm/`) in the browser over WebGPU. It starts only after the person agrees on a card that says what downloads, how big, from where, that it runs on this computer and that nothing written leaves it.
- **Where it shows.** "Analyse with AI" explains the lines the automation flagged and answers questions about them; the anonymous session has a real chat. Help now, the coping steps, "Write it out", "My plan for hard days" and "Tell the doctor on Earth" stay as they were. Both panels show "Rules this AI follows".
- **Its rules** (`src/ai-rules.js`, each with its source) act in three layers: before the model (risk words, and the private logs never go in), the system prompt built from the rules, and a check of every answer before it is shown. A held answer becomes a fixed text naming the rule; only a count is kept, never the text. The talk is not saved or sent and is forgotten when the panel closes.
- **Rules-only mode** when there is no WebGPU, no usable graphics chip, a failed download, the chip drops the model, or the switch "AI is working" is off. The panel says why in one sentence; everything else keeps working.
- **Seen in the answers** (limits of a 1.5B model): it does not always end by pointing to a person, it once suggested outside apps instead of only the program's cards, and once advised a nap "when there's no one on duty". None of these break the rules the code checks; they are why every answer is marked as a draft.

## The owner's decisions (open)

- **The exception to privacy.** Draft text in `crisisNotice()`, marked "TO CONFIRM" and not shown on the crew screen yet: in immediate danger, the program stops the talk, shows how to reach a person and tells the crew medical officer or flight surgeon that help is needed, without the words of the talk. Alternatives: only help on screen and ask first; or a one-line alert to a person chosen in advance.
- **Thresholds:** topic on 3 of the last 7 days (F1); "slower" at 10% from one's own runs (F3). Both placeholders.
- **The risk words** that open Help now (`RISK_PATTERNS` in `src/support.js`): a draft list. The same list guards what goes to the AI, with a draft Russian and Ukrainian translation (`RISK_DRAFT_RU_UK` in `src/ai-rules.js`). TO CONFIRM.
- **The AI** (all TO CONFIRM): which AI flies, and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct as the default; English only (a message in another script does not go to the model, and an answer in one is held); the AI may name only the program's own coping cards (open question 6); it never sends or calls (open question 1); it reads text only (open question 3); it does not judge the check-ins (open question 8).
- **The Earth break:** the order of the scenes and the mix as the default, rather than asking for a scene first; the starting volume of 50 percent. TO CONFIRM.
- **When the AI offer shows:** today only when the mind rule fires; whether a topic signal alone should show it.
- **The questionnaire** (PHQ-9, PHQ-8, GAD-7 or another): not added.
- **Where private writing is kept:** in the person's own file or only in this browser, and the passphrase lock as the next step.
- **Content:** the card and worksheet wording should be reviewed by a psychologist before real use; the calendar's real messages come from the families.
